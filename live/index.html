Nine complete source-derived variable fonts

Anatomy revision 2.1. Rebuilt junctions, terminal treatment, side carons, symbols and the descending thorn stem. This replaces the older 95-character trial.

Free typing in all nine families. Each encodes the declared 528 characters and includes 592 required glyphs, plus helpers. Weight, width and height adjust the font outlines. M/m has corrected shared construction.

Experimental display fonts. Detailed glyph review and optical tuning continue.
